Our story

More than a century of leadership.

Alpha Kappa Psi was founded at New York University in 1904 to develop principled business leaders. Today it is the oldest and largest co-ed professional fraternity in the world.

Beta Pi brought that mission to the University of Miami in 1941. Our brothers come from every major and background, united by a drive to grow professionally, give back, and lift each other up.

  1. 1904

    Alpha Kappa Psi is founded at New York University.

  2. 1941

    Beta Pi is chartered at the University of Miami on May 9.

  3. Today

    A co-ed chapter open to all majors, with brothers at top firms across the country.
