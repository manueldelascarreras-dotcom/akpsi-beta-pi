    Who we are

    Principled business leaders,
    made in Miami.

    Founded in 1904, Alpha Kappa Psi is the oldest and largest co-ed professional fraternity in the world. Beta Pi has carried that legacy at the University of Miami since 1941, and it is open to every major.
